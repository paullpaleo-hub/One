TikTok Master Machine

TikTok Master Machine is a private desktop application for macOS. It helps a single creator prepare their own videos for TikTok: it analyzes videos locally on the Mac, suggests captions and hashtags, and lets the creator review and approve every post.

After the creator approves a post, the app uploads the video to the creator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API. The creator completes or confirms the post in TikTok. Nothing is posted without explicit approval.

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner for their own TikTok account.
